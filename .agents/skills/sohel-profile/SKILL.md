## Evidence rules

- Keep verified facts, corroborated conclusions, strong inferences, and unknowns distinct.
- Preserve recorded date and title conflicts. Prefer the current authoritative source without deleting the older conflicting record.
- Treat first-party profiles and personal posts as self-reported unless another source corroborates them.
- Do not invent employers, ownership, technologies, dates, metrics, scale, impact, education, or management responsibility.
- Keep volatile counts with their recorded date or period. Refresh them from the public source when the user needs current figures.
- A repository, dependency, task title, or fork does not prove that Sohel built every part of it.
- Preserve the distinction between authored or owned work and code written with Cursor agents or other collaborators.
