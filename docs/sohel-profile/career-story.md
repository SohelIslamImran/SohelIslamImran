# Career story

## The reliable narrative

Sohel did not grow up around computers. He says his family had limited financial options, he passed SSC in 2020, and college was not affordable at that point. He first researched freelancing on a phone. In November 2020, his family used savings to buy a computer. He enrolled in Programming Hero the following month.

The learning method matters more than a generic "self-taught" label. Sohel says he watched lessons twice, first to understand and then to practice. He changed examples, built small projects, read other learners' questions, joined support sessions, and tried to solve problems before seeing the official answer. His public GitHub history from early 2021 and a restricted historical resume match that period of intensive project work.

The first professional break was an unpaid React Native internship at Tilleli in mid-2021. He took a short paid agency role at the same time, then continued with Tilleli. LinkedIn, a client recommendation, and restricted project-history review show that the work grew into a long-running React Native product role with Firebase, integrations, and app-store delivery.

Kuno marked the second major expansion. The public record starts with mobile application development in late 2023. From 2025 onward, authored changes move through frontend, backend, data, matching, AI-assisted workflows, authorization, infrastructure, testing, and deployment. The current public title is Lead Full Stack Engineer.

## Struggles supported by sources

- Limited family finances and no computer before late 2020.
- No conventional college route at the start of the career.
- An unpaid first internship and short, low-paid agency roles described as demanding.
- Repeated learning through documentation while working in unfamiliar systems.
- A later public account of officials questioning the "Software Engineer" title because Sohel had not attended college. This is personal context, not a resume item.

## Growth and turning points

1. **First computer and structured course, 2020.** This changed the path from a vague interest in freelancing to regular web-development practice.
2. **Internship and community referrals, 2021.** Helping other learners and sharing projects created the relationships that led to early opportunities.
3. **Long-running product responsibility at Tilleli.** Mobile UI work expanded into Firebase-backed features, integrations, and release operations.
4. **Kuno transition, 2023 to 2025.** The role moved from mobile application development to full-stack B2B systems.
5. **Lead title and broader scope, 2026.** Restricted authored-work records show contributions across product architecture, security, infrastructure, delivery, and operational tooling.

## Writing guidance

Tell this story as a sequence of concrete decisions and work. Avoid miracle language, exaggerated adversity, salary references, or claims that a lack of formal education proves superior ability. The useful message is that deliberate practice, community participation, and taking responsibility for larger parts of the system changed the available opportunities.
